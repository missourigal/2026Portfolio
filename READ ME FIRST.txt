SCHERRY SWEENEY — PORTFOLIO (local copy)
=========================================

HOW TO OPEN
   Double-click  index.html

   Everything links together from there. Keep this folder intact —
   the pages find each other by relative path, so moving index.html
   out on its own will break the links to the case studies.


WHAT'S IN HERE
   index.html ................ portfolio home
   quickbooks/ ............... Act! + QuickBooks case study
      assets/mockups/ ........ the 11 live mockups it embeds
      assets/fonts/ .......... fonts those mockups use
   flightpulse/ .............. GE Aviation FlightPulse case study
   act-mobile/ ............... Act! Mobile CRM case study
      assets/ ................ app screenshots


PASSWORD
   All three case studies are gated:   sweeney-portfolio-2026

   It's remembered per browser tab, so closing the tab means
   entering it again. When you send links to someone, put the
   password in the same email.


NEEDS INTERNET
   The pages load their typefaces (Fraunces, IBM Plex) from Google
   Fonts. Offline they still work perfectly, just in substitute
   fonts. Everything else — text, images, mockups, navigation —
   works with no connection at all.
