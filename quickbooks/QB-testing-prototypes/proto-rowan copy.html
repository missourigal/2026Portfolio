Connect QuickBooks Online

  1. 1What you get
  2. 2What moves, and the terms
  3. 3Authorize in QuickBooks
  4. 4Who can use it

Connect Act! to your QuickBooks Online company so quotes, invoices and payments stay in step without anyone typing them twice.

What you get

  • Accepted quotes become draft invoices, ready to review
  • Send invoices to QuickBooks Online without leaving Act!
  • Payments recorded in QuickBooks show against the right invoice here
  • Your contacts become QuickBooks customers the first time you invoice them

Before you connect

  • Your deals stay where they are. Your invoice history comes across. Quotes and deals you closed before connecting are not synced — Act! starts from here. Your recent invoices and the payments against them do come across, read-only, so the financial record is not empty on day one.
  • You’ll need an active QuickBooks Online subscription. Any plan works, though a few capabilities depend on which one you have. Connecting does not add a QuickBooks user or any QuickBooks cost — Act! connects as an app, not as a user.
  • Someone with QuickBooks admin access has to authorize it. That may not be you. You can start here, but you will be asked to sign in to QuickBooks as an administrator of your company.
  • Records this connection creates in Act! are saved under your name. If your Act! account is ever deactivated, the connection stops until another administrator sets it up again.

Next: what data moves, and the terms. Nothing connects until you authorize it in QuickBooks.

What moves

  • QuickBooks → Act! · now, once
    • Your product and service list, so quotes use the same items and prices as your books.
    • Your invoices, and the payments already made against them — as far back as you choose below. Act! reads these — it does not change them, and it does not send them anywhere.
      How far back should Act! look?
  • Act! → QuickBooks · from now onInvoices you send, and the customers they belong to.
  • QuickBooks → Act! · from now onPayments recorded against your invoices in QuickBooks, and their status. This keeps running for the invoices seeded above, not only the ones sent from Act!.

What Act! stores, and for how long

Act! keeps a copy of your invoices and the payments made against them — amounts, dates, status, and how each payment was made. It never stores card numbers, bank details, or anything else that could be used to take a payment. Your product and service list is copied across when you connect, and kept up to date as it changes in QuickBooks.

These copies live in your Act! database, in the same region as the rest of your Act! data. They stay for as long as you use the connection, and they remain if you disconnect — so your records do not develop a hole. What happens then is below.

Disconnecting

You can disconnect at any time, from this page. Disconnecting stops the two systems talking to each other: nothing new crosses in either direction.

Your QuickBooks records are never touched. What is in QuickBooks stays in QuickBooks — those are your financial records, and Act! does not remove them. Act! also holds on to your QuickBooks authorization for a short period, so reconnecting soon after does not mean signing in again; after that it is revoked and deleted.

On the Act! side, the invoices and payments we brought in are hidden, not thrown away. They stop updating and come off the screen, and Act! keeps them for [RETENTION PERIOD] afterwards. Reconnect inside that window and they come back where they were, with your links to quotes intact. After it, they are deleted — and reconnecting starts a fresh import instead.
